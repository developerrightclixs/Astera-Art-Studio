About us
Meet Colourful Imagination
Colourful Imagination by Astera Art was born from a simple passion: the joy of creating. 
Based in Kitchener, Ontario, we help people of all ages discover that they can make art, 
whatever they think their skill level is.  
We believe art isn’t only for “artists.” It’s for the child who loves to make things, the friend 
group celebrating a birthday, and the adult who has always wanted to learn to paint. Every 
session is relaxed, welcoming and focused on having fun while you learn.
What makes us different:
Step-by-step guidance : You’re never left guessing. We walk you through every stage.
All ages, all levels: From 6-year-olds to 70+, beginners are always welcome.
Real art education: Our classes teach the principles of art, not just how to copy a picture.
Led by passion: Everything we do comes from a love of creating and sharing it.
