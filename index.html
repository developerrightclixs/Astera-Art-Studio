About Us

Meet Colourful Imagination

Colourful Imagination by Astera Art was born from a simple passion: the joy of creating. Based in Kitchener, Ontario, we help people of all ages discover that they can make art, whatever they think their skill level is.

We believe art isn’t only for “artists.” It’s for the child who loves to make things, the friend group celebrating a birthday, and the adult who has always wanted to learn to paint. Every session is relaxed, welcoming and focused on having fun while you learn.

What makes us different:

  • Supportive guidance: A helping hand, with room for your own ideas.
  • All ages, all levels: Beginners are always welcome.
  • Real art education: Explore principles of art beyond copying a picture.
  • Led by passion: A love of creating and sharing it.

Make Yours Your Own.

There’s no single right way to create. At Colourful Imagination by Astera Art, we encourage you to explore your own ideas, follow your instincts, and see painting through your own perspective.

Take inspiration, experiment with colour, and let your personality come through. We’re here to guide you while giving your imagination the freedom to lead.

  • Confidence to try something new
  • A painting that’s uniquely yours
